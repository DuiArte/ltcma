Methodology

The standing validation protocol

  1. Optuna smart-search — ~5k–20k informed evals; structural priors fixed, not searched.
  2. Walk-forward + purged cross-validation with embargoed gaps (Bailey/López de Prado).
  3. Deflated Sharpe Ratio (DSR) reported alongside raw Sharpe (multiple-testing correction).
  4. Monte Carlo 100k–500k — trade-sequence bootstrap, entry jitter, +50% spread & slippage.
  5. Held-out test on the most recent slice (post-2024), never touched during selection.
  6. PBO (Probability of Backtest Overfitting, CSCV) alongside DSR.
  7. S&P 500 gate — must close-or-beat the index on at least one risk-adjusted axis.
  8. Survival bar — maximum drawdown under 10%.

Cost model is pessimistic retail (FxPro embedded per-bar spread as the floor, plus markup and stop slippage). Sizing is 1% fixed-fractional; under R-multiple accounting the Sharpe ratio is sizing-invariant. "n/a" means the metric was not computed in that report — it is never guessed.
