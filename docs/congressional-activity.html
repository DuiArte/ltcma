Method

Members must report a trade within 45 days. Amounts are the ranges they disclose. Dollar totals use each range's midpoint and are rough. Dates are keyed on the filing date, when the market can first know. Owner: self, SP spouse, JT joint, DC dependent child. Jurisdiction: JUR-1 means the member chairs a committee or subcommittee whose remit covers the stock's sector, JUR-2 is a ranking member, and JUR-3 a member. Only current roles count, and the committee-to-sector map is approximate. A cluster is 3 or more members trading the same stock the same way within 14 days, totalling $500K or more.
