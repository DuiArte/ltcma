A live view of the GBM equity book — every position measured against its cost basis and against the portfolio as a whole. Figures convert between Mexican pesos and US dollars at the current rate.

Display note: figures are scaled by a fixed constant for confidentiality — magnitudes are illustrative, not the real amounts; prices, returns and weights are exact. Positions and cost basis come from the broker statement (source of truth), re-priced live. Accounting: every position is measured against its cost basis — Return = (market value ÷ cost) − 1. For US-dollar holdings the peso P/L is split into Stock (the US price move at the exchange rate we bought at) and FX (the peso's move on the original cost); the two sum to Total P/L = market value − cost. The GBMF2 cash sleeve, FX positions and non-GBM bank cash are excluded from the equity book.
