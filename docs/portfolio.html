Display note: figures are scaled by a fixed constant for confidentiality — magnitudes are illustrative, not the real amounts; prices, returns and weights are exact. Positions and cost basis come from the broker statement (source of truth), re-priced live. Accounting: every position is measured against its cost basis — Return = (market value ÷ cost) − 1. For US-dollar holdings the peso P/L is split into Stock (the US price move at the exchange rate we bought at) and FX (the peso's move on the original cost); the two sum to Total P/L = market value − cost. (Stock here folds in a small price×FX interaction term, itemized separately in FX Attribution below.) The GBMF2 cash sleeve, FX positions and non-GBM bank cash are excluded from the equity book.

FX Attribution

For each US-dollar holding the unrealized peso P/L splits into Stock (US price move at the buy-date exchange rate), FX (the peso's move on the original cost) and a small Interaction cross term. The three sum exactly to Total = market value − cost — the same Total (and the same FX) as the Holdings table, which folds Interaction into its Stock column. Peso-native holdings (GMEXICO B) carry no FX leg. Values follow the currency toggle and are scaled; percentages are exact. ≈ = purchase FX estimated (no trade ticket).
