CRT (Candle Range Theory) Backtest — Report

Date: 2026-05-26 Author: Claude (Dispatch agent) For: Carlos Duarte Instruments: EURUSD, USDJPY, XAU/USD (Gold), XAG/USD (Silver) — FxPro M5 OHLC, 2016 → 2026-05-19 Risk model: 1% of equity per trade. Walk-forward IS = 2016–2022; OOS = 2023-01-01 → 2026-05-19. Cost model: Per-bar broker spread from the FxPro CSVs (half spread on entry, half on exit) + fixed slippage on stop fills (3 points EURUSD/USDJPY, 10 GOLD, 20 SILVER).

⚠️ Caveat on strategy rules (read first)

The audio your partner sent (4db4db10-Carloz.m4a) was not on disk at backtest time. I searched the entire mount — uploads folder empty, no .m4a/.mp3/.wav/.opus/.aac anywhere with that name or any matching pattern. I did not fabricate a transcription.

I implemented the standard ICT 3-candle Candle Range Theory as the documented baseline:

  1. C1 (HTF candle): defines range [LL, HH].
  2. C2 (next HTF candle): wicks beyond HH or LL but closes back inside [LL, HH] (the "manipulation" / liquidity sweep).
  3. C3 (entry candle): enter at C3 open opposite the sweep direction. Stop beyond the C2 wick. Configurable target (1R / 2R / 3R / opposite-range). Time stop after 4 HTF bars.

If your partner's CRT differs (different timeframes, different filters, different "ranges" — e.g. session-based Asia/London/NY treating sessions as C1/C2/C3), re-send the audio and I'll re-run. The harness is parameterized — swapping the entry rule and re-running takes minutes.

Brutal honest paragraph: Carlos asked for an honest verdict, not a curve-fit win. The pattern here is the same one you've seen before — a "system" that looks like a strategy is really a noisy edge that only becomes tradeable once you condition it on macro/regime context. CRT in isolation is a coin flip with worse-than-coin-flip win rate. The 3R asymmetric payoff drags performance positive on Gold and USDJPY at daily HTF, and trend alignment doubles that edge. None of the 384 regime variations cracked a Sharpe of 1. Your partner's CRT may have nuances I missed without the audio — but the standard 3-candle interpretation, on this data, with this cost model, does not beat your existing regime-adaptive multi-strategy work.

Method

Data

  • FxPro M5 tab-separated CSVs, Time / Open / High / Low / Close / TickVolume / RealVolume / Spread.
  • 2016 onwards (~770k M5 bars per FX pair, ~720k for metals).
  • Per-bar spread is in points; embedded directly in the cost model.

Strategy parameter grid (vanilla)

Parameter Values
HTF 1H, 4H, Daily
Take-profit 1R, 2R, 3R, opposite-range
Session filter (C3 open hour) all, London (8–13), NY (13–20)
HTF bias filter off, 200-EMA on HTF
Min sweep depth (frac of C1 range) 0.0, 0.1
Time stop 4 HTF bars
Direction both

= 144 combos × 4 instruments = 576 runs (some 0-trade due to filter intersections → 448 evaluated).

Regime overlay grid (Layer 2)

Base: Daily HTF, 2R + 3R, with/without 200-EMA bias, with/without min-sweep. Overlay (post-hoc filter on base trades):

Overlay Values
ATR regime any / low (ATR<P50 of trailing 252d) / high (ATR≥P50)
Trend regime any / with (entry direction matches daily 200-EMA)
Drop Monday & Friday entries off / on

= 24 overlays × 8 bases × 4 instruments = 768 runs (384 unique evaluated).

Risk + cost model

  • Risk: 1% of running equity per trade. PnL multiplier (1 + 0.01 × R). Compounding equity curve.
  • Spread: half spread (from FxPro Spread column at entry bar) subtracted from entry, half added back at exit.
  • Stop slippage: when stop is hit, fill is degraded by slip_points × point per instrument (3pt EURUSD/USDJPY, 10pt Gold, 20pt Silver). No slippage on TPs (limit fills).
  • No TP slippage assumed. Realistic for retail limit-order TPs but a tail-risk you should know about.

Walk-forward split

  • IS: 2016 → 2022 (7 years).
  • OOS: 2023 → 2026-05-19 (~3.5 years).

Parallelism

  • 2-core sandbox. Multiprocessing Pool(2) across instruments. Per-symbol HTF resamples cached. Total vanilla sweep: ~17s. Regime sweep: ~20s. Plenty of headroom for larger grids.

Spot-check concerns I'd flag: 1. The 2016–2018 segment is mostly flat or down. The strategy made its money in the 2019–2024 gold bull. If gold transitions to a bear or sideways regime, the long-skewed trend filter will hurt. 2. 2026 YTD already mildly negative — early signal that regime conditions may be shifting. 3. Sharpe 0.59, Calmar 0.33 — below the threshold most quant shops use to commit capital (>1 / >0.5). 4. Cost drag is real but small — 12% of edge eaten by spread+slippage. If your live broker spreads are wider than the FxPro historical median, this gets worse fast. 5. Stop slippage on Gold: I assumed 10 points (~$0.10). If your live broker slips $0.30–$0.50 on volatile sessions, that doubles cost drag and might kill the edge. 6. Silver winners have tiny n. 138–192 trades over 11 years is not enough to be confident the PF 1.5–1.7 is anything but lucky sampling. I'd require 500+ trades before deploying capital.

Next-up — Breedon & Ranaldo (SNB WP 2011-04 / SSRN 2099321)

Per Dispatch instructions, queueing the Breedon-Ranaldo local-hours FX rotation as the next strategy to test on the same framework.

Reference paper: "Intraday Patterns in FX Returns and Order Flow" — Mark Breedon & Angelo Ranaldo (SNB Working Paper 2011-04, also SSRN 2099321). Core finding: currencies tend to depreciate during their own country's trading hours and appreciate during foreign trading hours.

Implementation plan (same framework, ~1 day to build):

  1. Local-hours definition per currency: - EUR: 07:00–17:00 GMT - USD: 13:00–22:00 GMT - JPY: 00:00–09:00 GMT - GBP: 07:00–17:00 GMT
  2. Signal: Short the currency during its local hours, long it during foreign hours.
  3. Implementation on M5 data: Compute the intraday return of each currency-pair during the "local" hours of the base vs the quote. Trade direction = sign(quote-local-hours-return − base-local-hours-return).
  4. Cost model: Identical to CRT — broker spread per bar from FxPro CSVs.
  5. Variations to sweep: Hold periods (1h / 4h / 1d), with/without ATR regime, with/without trend filter, equal-weight vs return-weighted portfolio.
  6. Universe: EURUSD, USDJPY, GBPUSD, EURJPY, EURGBP (need to add GBP M5 if not already present — confirmed yes in fxpro_forex_m5/FxPro_M5_GBPUSD.csv).
  7. Walk-forward: Same IS 2016-2022 / OOS 2023-2026 split.
