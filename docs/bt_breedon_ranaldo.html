Method notes / honest caveats

  • Directions are fixed by the paper's structural prior (a currency depreciates during its own local working hours); they were NOT searched, to avoid doubling the hypothesis space. Raw signed-edge diagnostics are in results/sanity.txt. Notably the USDJPY Tokyo/US legs did not behave paper-consistently over 2014–2026 (the Tokyo-hours JPY-depreciation leg lost).
  • Timezone of the FxPro feed was empirically established as Europe/Bucharest (EET/EEST) — see code/TZ_FINDING.md. Session windows use DST-correct local time.
  • News calendar is approximate & offline: FOMC announcement dates (hardcoded) + NFP first-Friday. No live Forex-Factory feed was available; news_skip is a swept boolean built on this proxy. ECB/BoJ release-day skipping is NOT modelled.
  • Stop fills are detected on the M5 path within the holding window; intrabar high/low ordering is unknown so stop hits are taken at the level minus slippage.
  • Sharpe is sizing-invariant under R-multiple accounting, so position-sizing variants were evaluated via vol-scaling in the benchmark gate rather than in Optuna.
  • Deployable here = full Sharpe>0 AND DSR>0.95 AND PBO<0.5 AND held-out Sharpe>0 AND MC 5th-pct Sharpe>0 AND beats S&P on ≥1 axis.

Backtests run under a fixed validation protocol on pessimistic retail costs. Verdicts are research findings, not investment advice; past results do not guarantee future outcomes.
