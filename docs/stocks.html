Composite Picks

Every numeric variable from both stock pipelines — CFA/Buffett fundamentals (ROE, margins, FCF yield, margin of safety, growth, leverage, 12-1 momentum) and macro-signal sensitivities (earnings yield, β to rates / dollar / CAD, macro R²) — is z-scored across the 40-name universe, then combined into one composite score per ticker, weighted by each variable's correlation with realised forward returns. The variable direction is set by that correlation, not hand-picked. Tickers are ranked; the top 20 (★) are the picks. Each row shows the variables that contributed most (direction + relative size); the underlying weights are proprietary. Methodology & limitations

Composite. For each ticker in the union of the two pipelines, collect every numeric variable, z-score it cross-sectionally (missing → 0), and form a weighted sum. Weights = each variable's Pearson correlation with realised forward returns; the sign of that correlation sets the variable's direction. Normalised so the absolute weights sum to 1. Rank by composite; top 20 are the picks.

Forward-return proxy. The only realised forward-return history available across the universe is the post-FOMC 5-day drift, so weights are derived from each variable's cross-sectional correlation with the mean post-FOMC 5-day return. This is a genuine realised-return signal but a narrow one; a broader forward-return panel would sharpen the weights. If that history were absent the picker falls back to equal-weight z-scores (documented on the build).

Universe. The composite runs on the names that carry both fundamental and macro-signal variables (the macro-signal coverage set). The broader S&P 500 Buffett value screen stays available via the per-company tool.

Not advice. Educational research over a public universe. Free Yahoo Finance fundamentals are approximate; past forward-return relationships do not guarantee future returns.
